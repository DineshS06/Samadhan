Samadhan: turn constituency grievances into ranked projects

Samadhan groups citizen reports by category so one problem becomes a visible pattern across your constituency instead of an isolated complaint. Every project is scored on published, checkable criteria — no black box decides what matters in your area.

Reported categories

The most common grievances DARPG (Department of Administrative Reforms and Public Grievances) reports under: PM-KISAN instalments, the rural employment guarantee (MGNREGA), ration distribution, and health cover. Samadhan sorts these by category so constituents can see the real scale of issues in their area.

How a reported issue is ranked

Every grievance receives a published score composed of three equal-weight components: confirmed demand (40%), measured infrastructure deficit (40%), and severity (20%). Each component is auditable from published data, so any ranking decision can be checked and challenged by anyone. No black box determines what matters in your constituency.

The score has three published parts:

  • Demand: how many citizens report the same issue in your constituency
  • Infrastructure deficit: how many schools, hospitals, or utilities are below the published norm for the area
  • Severity: self-reported urgency by the citizen (1–5)

Read the full scoring methodology, or see the six-step workflow from report to ranked project.

What people report most often

  • PM-KISAN instalments not received
  • Ration distribution delays or errors
  • Rural employment guarantee (MGNREGA) work cards not issued
  • Rural health cover (AB-PMJAY) eligibility issues
  • Drinking water connection delays

Report a problem in your constituency

You can report a problem in any Indian language. Attach a location and any evidence you have (photos, documents, reference numbers). Your name and phone number stay private and are stored separately from the public record. You will receive a reference ID to track the report.

Common questions

Why has my scheme payment not been credited?

Published reporting identifies a small set of recurring causes: an incomplete e-KYC, an Aadhaar number not linked to the bank account, a name mismatch between Aadhaar and the bank record, land bought or inherited after 1 February 2019, and more than one claimant in the same family. Check your status on the official portal first, then report it here so the pattern is visible for your constituency.

How is an issue prioritised over another?

By the published 40-40-20 score, not by who asked loudest. Demand and infrastructure deficit each carry 40 percent, severity carries 20, and the components are published so any decision can be reviewed. No black box decides what matters in your constituency.

Who can see what I report?

The substance of a report is public so it can be counted. Your name, phone number and contact details are stored separately and are not shown on the public record. Read the privacy policy.
