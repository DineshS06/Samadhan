To: Hon'ble MP Office
From: Gajuwaka Citizens Welfare Association

Subject: Urgent — Primary Health Sub-Centre Deficiencies

We submit that the PHC sub-centre at Gajuwaka Block B lacks basic equipment and ambulance facility. During medical emergencies, patients are transported 18 km to the district hospital. Multiple deaths reported in transit last year.

We request immediate MPLADS intervention for facility upgrade.

Signed: 24 ward representatives
